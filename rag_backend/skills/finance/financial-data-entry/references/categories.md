# Financial Data Categories

## Revenue Categories (收入类别)
- 产品销售收入 - Product sales revenue
- 服务收入 - Service income
- 投资收益 - Investment income
- 利息收入 - Interest income
- 其他业务收入 - Other operating income
- 营业外收入 - Non-operating income

## Expense Categories (费用类别)
- 办公费用 - Office expenses
- 差旅费 - Travel expenses
- 人工成本 - Labor costs
- 市场营销 - Marketing expenses
- 研发费用 - R&D expenses
- 房租及物业 - Rent and property
- 水电费 - Utilities
- 通讯费 - Communication
- 物流运输 - Logistics and transportation
- 咨询顾问费 - Consulting fees
- 培训费 - Training expenses
- 折旧摊销 - Depreciation and amortization

## Asset Categories (资产类别)
- 固定资产 - Fixed assets (equipment, vehicles, buildings)
- 无形资产 - Intangible assets (patents, trademarks, software)
- 流动资产 - Current assets (cash, inventory, AR)

## Liability Categories (负债类别)
- 短期借款 - Short-term loans
- 长期借款 - Long-term loans
- 应付账款 - Accounts payable
- 应交税费 - Taxes payable
- 应付工资 - Wages payable
