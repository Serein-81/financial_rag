# Common Chinese Tax Terms Reference

## Tax Types (税种)
- 企业所得税 (Corporate Income Tax) — 25% standard, 15% for HI-Tech
- 增值税 (Value-Added Tax / VAT) — 13%/9%/6% rates
- 个人所得税 (Individual Income Tax) — progressive 3%-45%
- 印花税 (Stamp Duty) — various rates
- 房产税 (Property Tax) — 1.2% or 12%
- 关税 (Customs Duty) — varies by product
- 消费税 (Consumption Tax) — specific products only
- 城市维护建设税 (Urban Maintenance & Construction Tax) — 7%/5%/1%

## Key Tax Authorities
- 国家税务总局 (State Taxation Administration) — chinatax.gov.cn
- 财政部 (Ministry of Finance) — mof.gov.cn
- 省税务局 (Provincial Tax Bureau) — local branches
- 海关总署 (General Administration of Customs) — customs.gov.cn
