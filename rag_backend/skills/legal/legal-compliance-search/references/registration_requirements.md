# Company Registration Requirements

## Limited Liability Company (有限责任公司)
- Minimum 1 shareholder (natural person or legal entity)
- Registered capital: No minimum (except for special industries)
- Articles of Association
- Registered address (physical office address required)
- Legal representative (chairman or manager)
- Supervisor (or supervisory board for larger companies)

## Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise (外商独资企业)
- At least one foreign shareholder
- Registered capital should match business scale
- Business scope must be clearly defined
- May require approvals for restricted industries (Negative List)
- Foreign Investment Negative List must be checked

## Joint Venture (合资企业)
- At least one Chinese and one foreign shareholder
- Joint venture contract required
- Registered capital and contribution ratio agreed in contract
- Board of directors structure defined in contract

## Required Documents (all types)
1. Company name pre-approval (企业名称预先核准)
2. Articles of Association (公司章程)
3. Shareholder identification documents
4. Registered address proof (房产证复印件 + 租赁合同)
5. Legal representative ID and photo
6. Company seal registration (公章备案)

## Registration Steps
1. Name pre-approval (1-3 business days)
2. Submit registration application (3-5 business days)
3. Obtain business license (营业执照)
4. Company seal engraving and filing (1-2 business days)
5. Tax registration (税务登记) — within 30 days
6. Social insurance registration (社保登记) — within 30 days
7. Bank account opening (银行开户)
8. Foreign exchange registration (for foreign-invested enterprises)
